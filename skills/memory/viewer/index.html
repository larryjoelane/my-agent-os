Reading the graph

Each node is a stored memory, sized by the total weight of its edges. Each link joins two memories that came back together in the same search. That's Hebbian "fire together, wire together" strengthening.

Every co-retrieval moves an edge 30% of the way to a ceiling of 1, so strong edges level off instead of growing forever. Unused edges decay with a 14-day half-life. Stored is the weight on disk at the last co-retrieval. Decayed is that weight after decaying to the as-of date.
